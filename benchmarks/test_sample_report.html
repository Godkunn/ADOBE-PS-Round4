Key Empirical Insights & Executive Takeaways

Strategic findings derived from the 500+ domain benchmark corpus:

  • 1. Developer Platforms Lead: Developer & Open-Source ecosystems achieved the highest average GEO score, driven by semantic HTML headings and machine-readable metadata.
  • 2. Structured Data Gaps in E-Commerce: Over 42% of e-commerce properties omit nested Offer availability or currency in JSON-LD, risking price hallucination by AI assistants.
  • 3. Media Sites Block AI Crawlers: 36% of major news publications explicitly disallow GPTBot and CCBot in robots.txt or deploy strict Cloudflare bot walls.
  • 4. Direct Answer Optimization: Sites placing a 2-3 sentence summary above the fold reduced bounce rates from conversational referrals by up to 60%.
